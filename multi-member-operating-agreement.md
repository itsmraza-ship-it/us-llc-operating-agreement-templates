(a) **Company option.** For **[NUMBER, e.g., thirty (30)]** days after the Transfer Notice, the Company may elect to purchase all of the offered interest on the same terms.

(b) **Member option.** If the Company does not elect to purchase all of the offered interest, the remaining Members may, for **[NUMBER, e.g., thirty (30)]** additional days, elect to purchase all of the offered interest pro rata (or as they otherwise agree) on the same terms.

(c) **Third-party sale.** If neither the Company nor the Members elect to purchase all of the offered interest, the Transferring Member may complete the described Transfer to the identified transferee within **[NUMBER, e.g., ninety (90)]** days, on terms no more favorable to the transferee, provided the transferee executes a joinder. If not completed within that period, the interest shall again be subject to this Section.

(e) **[OTHER EVENTS, IF ANY]**.

**Section 10.2 Winding Up.** Upon dissolution, a liquidating trustee selected by Members holding a majority of the Percentage Interests of the remaining Members shall wind up the Company in accordance with applicable law, in the following order:
