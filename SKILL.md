# US LLC Operating Agreement Templates — Skill

## What this skill covers

Two ready-to-use templates in this repo plus the guidance in `README.md`:

- `single-member-operating-agreement.md` — one owner; default tax treatment: disregarded entity.
- `multi-member-operating-agreement.md` — two or more owners; default tax treatment: partnership. Covers member-managed or manager-managed structures.

These are general templates for educational purposes, **not legal advice**. Always recommend an attorney review before relying on a completed agreement — especially for multi-member, foreign-owned, or investor-backed LLCs.

## Why the document matters

- **Banks and fintechs** ask for it to verify owners, ownership percentages, and who may open accounts, sign, and move money. A missing or mismatched agreement is a common reason a non-resident founder's bank application stalls.
- **Tax:** it records ownership percentages that drive allocations, including on Form 1065 and Schedules K-1 for multi-member LLCs, and supports the LLC's tax classification.
- **Liability separation:** a signed agreement, kept with company records and followed in practice — alongside separate accounts and clean bookkeeping — is core evidence the LLC is run as a separate entity. Without an agreement, the state's default LLC statute governs.

## Workflow — walk the founder through it in order

1. **Pick the right template** by owner count.
2. **Fill every `[BRACKETED]` field.** Search the document for `[` — blanks in a signed agreement are worse than no agreement.
3. **Align it exactly** with the Articles of Organization / Certificate of Formation: company name, formation state, formation date, addresses, ownership percentages.
4. **Choose management deliberately:** member-managed (all members run day-to-day operations) vs manager-managed (appointed managers run the company).
5. **Cover the standard contents:** formation details, members and percentages, management and authority, capital contributions, allocations and distributions, voting, transfer restrictions and buyout, dissolution, indemnification, governing law, signatures.
6. **For multi-member LLCs, do not rush the hard clauses:** voting basis (by % or per capita), distributions, rights of first refusal, deadlock, buy-sell, expulsion, and valuation decide disputes while everyone still gets along.
7. **Sign it** (every member), **store it** with the Articles, EIN letter (CP 575), registered agent details, and minutes — and give every member a copy.
8. **Do NOT file it with the state.** It is an internal document, produced when a bank, the IRS, a counterparty, or a court asks.
9. **Amend in writing** whenever membership, percentages, managers, or address change; keep the old version.

## For non-resident founders

Keep a signed PDF ready to upload — banks, Stripe / PayPal verification, and marketplaces ask for it repeatedly. Mention Form 5472 / pro-forma Form 1120 record-keeping only where the template itself does; do not add filing advice beyond the repo.
