**Section 2.2 Membership Interest.** The Member's interest in the Company (the "**Membership Interest**") represents the Member's entire right, title, and interest in the Company, including rights to distributions, allocations, and management as set forth in this Agreement.

**Section 2.4 Limited Liability.** The Member shall not be personally liable for any debts, obligations, or liabilities of the Company solely by reason of being a member of the Company, to the fullest extent permitted by law.

## ARTICLE III — MANAGEMENT

**Section 3.1 Member-Managed.** The Company shall be **member-managed**. The Member shall have full, exclusive, and complete authority to manage, control, and operate the business and affairs of the Company and to make all decisions regarding the Company.

**Section 3.2 Authority of the Member.** Without limiting Section 3.1, the Member is authorized, on behalf of and in the name of the Company, to:

(a) open, maintain, and close bank, brokerage, payment-processor, and other financial accounts, and designate authorized signers on such accounts;

(b) enter into, execute, and deliver contracts, leases, notes, and other instruments;

(c) buy, sell, lease, license, and otherwise deal in real and personal property;

(d) borrow money, incur indebtedness, and grant security interests in Company assets;

(e) hire and terminate employees, agents, and independent contractors, and engage attorneys, accountants, and other advisors;

(f) commence, defend, compromise, and settle claims and litigation; and

(g) take any other action necessary, convenient, or incidental to the purposes of the Company.

**Section 3.3 Officers and Agents.** The Member may appoint one or more officers, managers, or agents of the Company, with such titles, authority, and duties as the Member may determine, and may remove any such person at any time, with or without cause.

**Section 3.4 Reliance by Third Parties.** Any third party dealing with the Company may rely conclusively on the authority of the Member (and of any officer or agent appointed under Section 3.3 acting within the scope of their appointment) to bind the Company, without duty to inquire further.

**Section 3.5 Other Activities.** The Member may engage in other business ventures of any nature, whether or not competitive with the Company, and neither the Company nor any other person shall have any claim or right in or to such ventures or their proceeds by reason of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE IV — CAPITAL CONTRIBUTIONS

**Section 4.1 Initial Contribution.** The Member has contributed the initial capital set forth in Section 2.1, which has been received by the Company.

**Section 4.2 Additional Contributions.** The Member may, but shall not be obligated to, make additional capital contributions to the Company at any time and in any amount. Any additional contribution shall be recorded in the Company's books and in an updated Exhibit A or written record maintained with this Agreement.

**Section 4.3 No Interest; No Withdrawal.** Capital contributions shall not bear interest. The Member shall not be entitled to withdraw or demand the return of any capital contribution except upon distributions made in accordance with Article V or upon dissolution in accordance with Article IX.

**Section 4.4 No Personal Liability for Company Debts.** No capital contribution obligation under this Article shall make the Member personally liable for the debts or obligations of the Company beyond the Member's agreed contributions.

---

## ARTICLE V — ALLOCATIONS AND DISTRIBUTIONS

**Section 5.1 Allocations.** For so long as the Company has a single member, all items of income, gain, loss, deduction, and credit of the Company shall be allocated to the Member.

**Section 5.2 Tax Treatment.** The Member intends that the Company be treated as a disregarded entity for United States federal income tax purposes (or, if the Member is a foreign person or entity, in accordance with applicable Treasury Regulations and the Member's elections), unless the Member elects in writing for the Company to be taxed as a corporation. The Member may make any tax election permitted by law, including under Treasury Regulation Section 301.7701-3.

**Section 5.3 Distributions.** Distributions of cash or property may be made to the Member at such times and in such amounts as the Member determines, provided that no distribution shall be made if, after giving effect to the distribution, the Company would be insolvent or unable to pay its debts as they become due, or if the distribution would violate applicable law or any obligation of the Company.

**Section 5.4 No Salary for Membership.** The Member shall not receive a salary or guaranteed payment solely by reason of being the Member; the Member's return as owner shall be through distributions. Compensation for services, if any, shall be separately agreed in writing.

**Section 5.5 Withholding.** The Company may withhold and remit from distributions any amounts required to be withheld by law, and any amount so withheld shall be treated as distributed to the Member.

**Section 6.2 Bank Accounts; Separation of Funds.** All Company funds shall be held in accounts in the name of the Company and shall not be commingled with the personal funds of the Member. The Member shall conduct the affairs of the Company as a separate entity.

**Section 6.4 Annual Filings.** The Member shall cause the Company to make all recurring state filings required to keep the Company in good standing, including annual or periodic reports, if any, in the State of **[STATE OF FORMATION]**.

## ARTICLE VIII — TRANSFER OF MEMBERSHIP INTEREST

**Section 8.1 Permitted Transfers.** The Member may sell, assign, transfer, gift, pledge, or otherwise dispose of all or any part of the Membership Interest, in whole or in part, at any time, with or without consideration.

**Section 8.2 Admission of Transferee.** A transferee of the entire Membership Interest who is acquiring the interest by a transfer permitted under Section 8.1 shall be admitted as the sole member of the Company upon execution of a written amendment to, or restatement of, this Agreement reflecting the transfer. A transferee of a partial interest shall be admitted as a member only if the Member (or successor Member) so agrees in writing; otherwise the transferee shall be a mere assignee entitled only to the economic rights transferred, with no right to manage or vote.

**Section 8.3 Effect on Structure.** If, as a result of any transfer or admission, the Company has two or more members, the members shall amend and restate this Agreement in a multi-member form addressing, at a minimum, voting, allocations, distributions, transfer restrictions, and buyout mechanics.

**Section 8.4 Invalid Transfers.** Any purported transfer in violation of this Article shall be null and void and shall not be recognized by the Company.

## ARTICLE IX — DISSOLUTION AND WINDING UP

**Section 9.1 Events of Dissolution.** The Company shall be dissolved upon the occurrence of any of the following:

(a) the written election of the Member to dissolve the Company;

(b) the sale or other disposition of all or substantially all of the Company's assets;

(c) the death, incapacity, bankruptcy, or dissolution of the Member, unless a successor member is admitted in accordance with Article VII or VIII and the Company is continued;

(d) any event causing the Company to be dissolved under the laws of the State of **[STATE OF FORMATION]**; or

(e) **[OTHER DISSOLUTION EVENTS, IF ANY]**.

**Section 9.2 Winding Up.** Upon dissolution, the Member (or a liquidating trustee appointed by the Member or the Member's successor) shall wind up the affairs of the Company in accordance with applicable law, in the following order of priority:

(a) payment of debts and liabilities of the Company (including amounts owed to the Member in the Member's capacity as a creditor, if any), and establishment of reasonable reserves;

(b) distribution of any remaining assets to the Member.

**Section 9.3 Articles of Dissolution.** Upon completion of the winding up, the Member shall file Articles of Dissolution / a Certificate of Cancellation with the Secretary of State of **[STATE OF FORMATION]** and take all other actions required by law to terminate the Company.

**Section 9.4 No Deficit Restoration.** The Member shall have no obligation to restore any deficit capital account balance upon liquidation.

## ARTICLE X — INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY

**Section 10.1 Indemnification.** To the fullest extent permitted by the laws of the State of **[STATE OF FORMATION]**, the Company shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Member, and any officer, manager, or agent appointed under Section 3.3 (each, an "**Indemnified Person**"), from and against any and all claims, damages, losses, liabilities, judgments, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or in connection with the business and affairs of the Company or such person's service to the Company, except to the extent caused by such person's fraud, willful misconduct, or knowing violation of law.

**Section 10.2 Advancement of Expenses.** The Company shall advance reasonable expenses (including attorneys' fees) incurred by an Indemnified Person in defending any proceeding in advance of its final disposition, subject to an undertaking by the Indemnified Person to repay such amounts if it is ultimately determined that the Indemnified Person is not entitled to indemnification.

**Section 10.3 Limitation of Liability.** No Indemnified Person shall be liable to the Company for any act or omission taken in good faith in connection with the business of the Company, except for fraud, willful misconduct, or a knowing violation of law.

**Section 10.4 Insurance.** The Company may purchase and maintain insurance on behalf of any Indemnified Person against any liability asserted against such person in connection with the Company's activities, whether or not the Company would have the power to indemnify such person against such liability under this Article.
